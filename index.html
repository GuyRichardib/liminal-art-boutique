Which drawing style is right for me?

Intricate scenes contain smaller details for a longer session. Scene collections focus on places and stories. Bold & Easy offers simpler shapes and bolder outlines. Use the filters and films to compare.

Are these printed books or digital downloads?

These are physical paperbacks, sold on Amazon. This site offers selected previews, not downloadable coloring interiors.

Can I see the actual artwork before buying?

Yes. Choose a book in the film player to see selected real pages. The two details below the player are cropped and visibly watermarked. Digital colors in a film are a palette preview.

Where can I find the price and available editions?

Use the Amazon link for the book you like. Current prices, delivery and availability depend on your marketplace.
