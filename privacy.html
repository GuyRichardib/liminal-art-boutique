Privacy & your choices

You decide what leaves this page.

Coloring a page

When you color a drawing on this site, everything happens in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. "Save my page" creates an image on your device; sharing it is your choice.

If you subscribe to Palette Fridays

A small Palette Fridays panel may appear once while you browse. If you subscribe, your email address and the page you subscribed from are sent to MailerLite, the service that sends the Friday emails. You join only after confirming your address from the email MailerLite sends you, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

If you close the panel or subscribe, your browser remembers that choice on your device (local storage), so the panel does not come back. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Delivery

GitHub Pages serves this site and may process technical request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver and protect it. Fonts, images and films come from this site; the pages load no font or script from another company's servers. There is no advertising pixel, no analytics and no cookie of our own.

Links

Amazon and MailerLite apply their own policies when you follow their links.
